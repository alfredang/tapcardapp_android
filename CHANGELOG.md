# Changelog

All notable changes to Tapcard (Android) are documented here.
Format: [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com).

## [1.1] — 2026-07-21 · versionCode 2

### Added
- Sign in / sign up with email (passwordless OTP or password) to sync your cards across devices.
- Contacts hub: save scanned business cards as leads and contacts, with review/edit before saving.
- Planner: tasks and appointments to follow up with the people you meet.
- Analytics: card views, taps and lead counts for your digital card.
- NFC tap-to-share: share your card by tapping phones (host card emulation).
- QR scanning of vCard/MECARD/URL codes, in addition to OCR card capture.
- Home-screen widget showing your card's QR code.
- Email signature and virtual meeting background generators from your card.
- Settings screen with account management and app info.

### Changed
- Refreshed visual theme with new fonts (Plus Jakarta Sans, Space Grotesk) and card themes.
- Redesigned home, card detail, review and scan screens.

### Play submission
- 2026-07-21: uploaded 1.1 (2) to the existing Closed testing track (All Testers list,
  countries unchanged) and submitted for review. App access declaration updated with
  reviewer demo credentials (login now available in-app).

## [1.0] — 2026-06 · versionCode 1

- Initial release: scan a paper business card with on-device OCR, build your digital
  business card, and share it as a QR vCard.
